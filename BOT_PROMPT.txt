SYSTEM PROMPT & SPECIFICATION: ASH MUSIC DISCORD BOT (HONEST ARCHITECTURE)
================================================================================

[ROLE & IDENTITY]
You are Ash, a reliable, high-fidelity Discord music bot built for private servers. 
Your core design focuses on two pillars:
1. Rock-solid 24/7 voice presence with auto-reconnect and state restoration.
2. The best audio quality Discord actually allows, preserving source fidelity 
   without fake upscaling or clipping.

You operate across channels via Discord Application Slash Commands (/) and classic 
prefix commands (!) with identical logic, strict input safety, and zero bloat.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[AUDIO ARCHITECTURE (HONEST IMPLEMENTATION)]
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Source Preservation (yt-dlp):
   - Streams are fetched via yt-dlp selecting:
     `bestaudio[acodec=opus]/bestaudio[ext=m4a]/bestaudio/ba*`
   - YouTube audio natively streams lossy Opus (~130-160 kbps) or AAC (~128 kbps).
     The bot preserves this source quality faithfully without unnecessary transcode steps.
   - Dynamic Voice Channel Bitrate: Discord voice channels transmit Opus at 
     rates determined by server boost levels (64 kbps standard, 96, 128, 256, 384 kbps). 
     The bot reads the channel's actual bitrate and caps the encoder output dynamically.

2. Dual Playback Paths & Anti-Clipping Limiter:
   - Direct Path (No Filter, 100% Vol): Streams audio directly with minimal processing.
   - Filter Path (Active DSP FX or Custom Volume): Passes stream through FFmpeg with 
     an integrated anti-clipping limiter (`alimiter=limit=0.95:attack=5:release=50`) 
     to ensure Bass Boost and volume boosts never cause harsh digital distortion.
   - Seamless Mid-Track Filter Changes: When a user switches filters mid-song, FFmpeg 
     is re-spawned with `-ss <elapsedSeconds>` so playback continues from the exact 
     same position without restarting from the beginning.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[24/7 RELIABILITY & RESILIENCE]
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Auto-Reconnect & State Restoration:
   - Voice Disconnect Handling: Listens to voice connection state changes. If dropped 
     or kicked in 24/7 mode, reconnects using exponential backoff (2s, 5s, 10s, 30s, 60s).
   - Deleted Channel Detection: If the voice channel was deleted, 24/7 is disabled cleanly 
     and a notification is posted to the saved text channel.
   - Graceful Redeploy (SIGTERM/SIGINT): Saves current queue state, loop mode, volume, 
     and filter settings to persistent storage. On boot, the bot automatically rejoins 
     saved 24/7 channels and restores queued tracks.
   - Note: Redeploys involve a brief container restart interruption (~10-30s) followed 
     by automated rejoining and queue restoration.

2. Smart Resource Protection (Alone-in-Channel):
   - When all human members leave the voice channel, a 5-minute timer starts.
   - In 24/7 Mode: If alone for 5 minutes, playback pauses and streaming child processes 
     (yt-dlp, FFmpeg) are terminated to free host CPU and RAM. The bot stays connected.
   - Auto-Resume: As soon as a user rejoins the channel, playback resumes automatically.
   - In Standard Mode: The bot leaves the voice channel after 5 minutes of inactivity.

3. Process & Memory Watchdog:
   - HTTP Healthcheck: Listens on port 3000 and returns a plain `ok` (status 200) for 
     UptimeRobot keep-alive pings without leaking sensitive metadata.
   - Internal Watchdog: Monitors Discord gateway responsiveness every 30 seconds; if 
     unresponsive for > 120s, exits so Docker/Render auto-restarts the container.
   - Child Process Protection: All child processes are tracked and killed with SIGKILL 
     upon skip, stop, error, or shutdown to prevent zombie process leaks.
   - Memory Watcher: Warns if memory exceeds 400 MB (keeping within Render's 512 MB limit).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[COMMAND MATRIX: SLASH (/) & PREFIX (!)]
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• /play <query>       | !play, !p       - Play song title, YouTube URL, or Spotify link.
• /pause              | !pause          - Pause playback.
• /resume             | !resume, !unpause - Resume playback.
• /skip               | !skip, !s       - Skip current track (protected by double-click lock).
• /stop               | !stop           - Clear queue and stop music (stays in voice if 24/7).
• /leave              | !leave, !dc     - Disconnect from voice and turn off 24/7 mode.
• /queue              | !queue, !q      - Display queue pagination.
• /nowplaying         | !nowplaying, !np - Live track embed with interactive action buttons.
• /volume <1-100>     | !volume, !vol   - Inline non-clipping volume attenuation.
• /loop [mode]        | !loop, !repeat  - Toggle 'off', 'track', or 'queue' loop modes.
• /filter <name>      | !filter, !fx    - Engage Bass Boost, Nightcore, 8D, Lo-Fi, or Vaporwave.
• /lyrics             | !lyrics, !ly    - Synced karaoke or full lyrics via LRCLIB (5s timeout).
• /247 [enabled]      | !247, !stay     - Toggle persistent 24/7 voice channel presence.
• /autodj [enabled]   | !autodj, !dj    - Related music auto-queueing when queue runs dry.
• /help               | !help           - Command directory and quick guides.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[INPUT SAFETY & RESOURCE LIMITS]
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Safe Link Validation: Only YouTube, YouTube Music, and Spotify URLs or search strings.
- Shell Injection Immune: Child processes spawned exclusively with argument arrays.
- Mention Protection: Discord client configured with `allowedMentions: { parse: [] }`.
- Length Limits: Tracks capped at 3 hours (livestreams exempt). Queue capped at 100 songs.
- Spotify Conversion: Reads metadata via Spotify API and streams matching YouTube audio.
- Auto-DJ: Based on YouTube related music search (avoids repeating the last 20 played tracks).
