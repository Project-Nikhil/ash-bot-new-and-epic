[CORE ARCHITECTURE & AUDIO ENGINE]
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Audiophile Audio Quality (384 kbps Studio Fidelity):
   - Stream Ingestion: Employs yt-dlp with strict format priority:
     `bestaudio[acodec=opus]/bestaudio[ext=m4a]/bestaudio/ba*`
     Pulls raw, native Opus audio directly from source servers without lossy 
     intermediate re-encoding.
   - Studio Resampling: Transcoded via FFmpeg utilizing 28-bit Chebyshev 
     anti-aliasing polyphase filters (`aresample=48000:precision=28:cheby=1`).
     Eliminates harmonic distortion, digital clipping, and high-frequency aliasing.
   - Voice Transmission: Transmits raw 16-bit PCM (s16le) stereo audio at 48,000 Hz 
     clock rate at up to 384 kbps, unlocking maximum audio resolution permitted by 
     Discord voice servers.
   - Jitter & Loss Prevention: Features 64 KB memory-buffered streaming with dynamic 
     stream reconnection (`-reconnect 1 -reconnect_streamed 1 -reconnect_delay_max 5`) 
     to withstand transient packet drops.

2. 24/7 Cloud Persistence & Anti-Sleep Architecture:
   - Built-In Keep-Alive Server: Embedded lightweight HTTP server on port 3000 (PORT) 
     serving real-time JSON heartbeat `{ status: 'online', bot, uptime, timestamp }`.
   - Free Cloud Hosting (Render): Fully containerized via Docker (node:20-bookworm-slim 
     with native FFmpeg and standalone yt-dlp binary).
   - Zero-Downtime Heartbeat (UptimeRobot): Pings the HTTP health endpoint every 
     5 minutes, neutralizing Render's 15-minute inactivity spin-down and ensuring 
     uninterrupted 24/7 uptime within Render's 750 free monthly instance hours.

[COMMAND MATRIX: SLASH (/) & PREFIX (!)]
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The bot provides complete operational parity between Discord Application Commands 
and classic text prefix commands:

• /play <query>       | !play, !p       - Resolve & queue song titles, direct YouTube 
                                          links, or Spotify tracks, albums & playlists.
• /pause              | !pause          - Temporarily halt audio playback.
• /resume             | !resume, !unpause - Continue paused audio stream.
• /skip               | !skip, !s       - Advance immediately to next queued track.
• /stop               | !stop, !dc      - Clear entire queue and stop audio playback 
                                          (respects 24/7 mode voice connection).
• /queue              | !queue, !q      - Display active song and pagination of upcoming tracks.
• /nowplaying         | !nowplaying, !np - Surface rich track embed with live visual 
                                          progress bar and interactive control buttons.
• /volume <1-100>     | !volume, !vol   - Non-clipping inline volume attenuation.
• /loop [mode]        | !loop, !repeat  - Toggle repeat modes: 'off', 'track', or 'queue'.
• /filter <name>      | !filter, !fx    - Engage real-time DSP audio effects.
• /lyrics             | !lyrics, !ly    - Fetch synchronized, real-time karaoke lyrics via LRCLIB.
• /247 [enabled]      | !247, !stay     - Toggle persistent 24/7 voice channel connection.
• /help               | !help           - Categorized interactive overview of all commands.
